Everything a chama does

One app for the whole group.

Set your own rules once: the monthly amount, the deadline, the fine, the loan interest. The app follows them for every member, every month.

📅

Monthly contributions

See who has paid each month, who paid late and who still owes, for one member or the whole chama.

📩

Reads the bank message

Copy the KCB or Paybill SMS and the app fills in the amount, the reference and who paid. The same payment can never be recorded twice.

⏰

Fines, automatically

Missed the deadline? The fine is added overnight. Paid on time but confirmed late? It's cancelled for you.

💸

Loans that add up

Limits based on each member's savings, a guarantor who accepts in the app, a monthly repayment plan and interest worked out exactly.

📄

Statements & PDFs

Every member sees a full statement with a running balance and can download it as a PDF to share on WhatsApp.

👥

Roles for officials

Chair, Secretary and Treasurer each get the tools they need. Ordinary members see only their own details.

🧾

Expenses, approved by all

Registration, paperwork, travel: every shilling the chama spends is recorded, and it only counts once every official has approved it.

📲

Bring in your WhatsApp records

Paste your old "money received" list and the app matches each name to a member, so months before the app aren't lost.

How it works

Up and running in an afternoon.

The chairperson registers the chama

Set the monthly amount, the deadline, the fine and the loan rules. They become the admin.

Officials add the members

Name and phone number. Each member gets a one-time code to create their own password. No SMS fees.

Members pay as usual

Through your chama's Paybill or bank account. The money never passes through the app.

The treasurer records, another official confirms

Paste the bank message and it's matched to the member. A second official confirms it before it counts.

Built for trust

Records nobody can quietly change.

A chama runs on trust. The app is built so that trust doesn't depend on one person.

✓

Two people for every payment

One official records it and a different official confirms it. Nobody can confirm their own deposit, and every expense needs all the officials to agree.

✓

Nothing is ever deleted

A mistake is fixed with a correcting entry and a reason, shown on the statement for everyone to see.

✓

Your money stays in your bank

The app keeps the records. Your money stays in your chama's own account, so the app can never touch it.

✓

Privacy between members

Members see their own statement and the group totals, never each other's phone numbers or next of kin.

Questions

Good questions to ask.

Does the app hold our money?

No. Members keep paying into your chama's own Paybill or bank account. The app only keeps the records, so nobody can withdraw money through it.

Our records are on WhatsApp and in a notebook. Can we bring them in?

Yes. The admin can paste a WhatsApp "money received" list for past months, and the app matches each name to a member.

Can we use our own rules?

Yes. Your chama sets the monthly amount, the deadline, the late fine, the loan interest, how much a member can borrow and how many guarantors a loan needs.

How are chama expenses handled?

Any official records the expense: what it was for, the amount and the date. Every other official then approves it in the app. Only when all of them agree does it come off the chama's balance, and one "no" cancels it.

What if a member pays the wrong amount?

The app shares it out for you: fines first, then unpaid months, then loan instalments. Anything extra goes to that member's savings.

Does every member need a smartphone?

Members with an Android phone get the full app. Officials can still record payments for members who don't have one, so their records are kept the same way.
